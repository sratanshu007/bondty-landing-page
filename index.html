How it works

Three steps and you’re talking.

Log in with your mobile number and an OTP. Then top up your wallet and pick someone to talk to.

  1. Top up your wallet

    Pay with UPI, card or netbanking through Razorpay. Every pack adds bonus coins to your wallet.

  2. Choose a listener

    Compare ratings, languages, topics and per-minute rates. Or tap Random Connect to reach whoever is free.

  3. Talk, billed by the minute

    The timer starts when the conversation does. When your balance runs out, the session ends on its own with no extra charges.

Ways to talk

However you feel comfortable.

Chat

Voice call

Safety

Your conversations stay yours.

Bondty is for conversation only. Dating, money transfers and asking for personal numbers are not allowed.

  • Numbers and links hidden automaticallyIf someone types a phone number or link in chat, that part is hidden.
  • Verified listenersOur team checks every partner’s ID and profile before approving them.
  • Report and block in one tapIf something feels wrong, report it from inside the session. Our team reviews reports within 24 hours.
  • 18+ onlyThe app is only for people aged 18 and over.

FAQ

About payments and conversations.

How am I charged?

Each listener’s rate is shown in coins per minute. The timer starts when the conversation starts. A partial minute counts as a full minute. 1 coin = ₹1.

What if the listener doesn’t accept my request?

You aren’t charged. Nothing leaves your wallet until the conversation starts.

What if my balance runs out mid-session?

The session ends automatically. You get a warning when your balance is low, and you can top up to keep talking.

Can the listener see my number?

No. Listeners only see your first name, and you can use any name you like instead of your real one.

Can I get a refund on a gift?

No. Gifts are entirely optional, and gifts that have been sent can’t be refunded.

Is this therapy?

No. Bondty is for conversation and feeling lighter. For medical, legal or financial decisions, always consult a qualified professional.
