About Bondty

Bondty is a conversation platform for people who want to be heard. Many people carry stress about exams, work, family or loneliness and have no one to talk to without being judged. Bondty connects them, privately and safely, with verified Listeners and Experts over chat, voice call and video call.

Users pay only for the minutes they talk, from a prepaid wallet. Listeners and Experts are independent partners who go through ID and profile verification before they can take sessions, and earn for every minute they spend helping someone.

Bondty is built with safety first: numbers and links are hidden in chat, every session can be reported in one tap, and the Platform is for adults aged 18 and over only. Bondty is not a dating service and does not provide medical or therapy services.
